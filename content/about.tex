\section{Hello}

I'm Nick. I work on graphs and algorithms, and this is where I write things
down that don't fit anywhere else --- half-finished ideas, notes on papers I've
read, and the occasional worked example.

\section{Why LaTeX}

Because I'd rather write $\bigO{m\sqrt{n}}$ than fight a rich text editor, and
because the things I want to explain usually need a diagram with real
mathematical typesetting in it.

Every page on this site is a \LaTeX{} document. A build step converts it to
HTML with MathML for the equations, and compiles each TikZ picture to SVG. The
practical upshot is that lifting a section out of a paper and putting it here
is mostly a copy-and-paste.
