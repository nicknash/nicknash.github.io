\begin{abstract}
In Summer 2025, I looked for an easy graph algorithm to asymptotically improve.
After weeks of work, and long peer review, it ended up as a published paper
(LINK).
In Summer 2026, \href{https://chatgpt.com/share/6a8434d4-c278-83ed-aeb1-f2f0cd1bdb62}{AI}
came up with all the ideas of the paper in about two hours. I then considered
automating this entire process: find \textit{all} ``easy'' graph algorithms,
and improve them with AI. At first this idea seemed cool, after chatting to
some people and a bit of reading, I realised I might instead create an
\textit{intellectual wasteland}.
\end{abstract}
